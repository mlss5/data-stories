Every dollar of foreign aid tells two stories: who gives, and who receives. This chart maps flows from the five largest donors each year to the countries that depend on that generosity most. Drag the slider to watch Ukraine’s rise from minor recipient to the world’s largest aid destination after 2022 — and to see how that shift displaced traditional recipients across Africa and South Asia.

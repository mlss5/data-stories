The Rise and Fall of Foreign Aid

ODA donor trends, 2010–2025

Data Story  ·  OECD DAC  ·  Official Development Assistance

Foreign aid from 28 of 33 DAC member countries grew between 2019 and 2023 — then retracted sharply. In 2025, the United States cut overseas development assistance by nearly 57%, the largest single-year reduction by any donor on record.

Gross ODA disbursements, grant-equivalent basis, constant 2022 USD billions. 2024: final OECD release (Dec 2025). 2025: preliminary (Apr 2026), shown as open circles. Dotted lines mark the 2015 refugee crisis and 2022 Ukraine war onset. Sector breakdowns for all 33 donors are author estimates based on OECD CRS aggregate reporting.

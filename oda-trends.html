The Rise and Fall of Foreign Aid

ODA donor trends, 2010–2025

ODA

Published

Data Story  ·  OECD DAC  ·  Official Development Assistance

Foreign aid from wealthy nations rose five years running — then collapsed. In 2025, the United States cut overseas assistance by nearly 57%, the largest single-year reduction by any donor on record. Germany now leads the world.

Gross ODA disbursements, grant-equivalent basis, constant 2022 USD billions. 2024: final OECD release (Dec 2025). 2025: preliminary (Apr 2026), open circles.

Key numbers

US ODA change 2025
−56.9%
Largest reduction by any donor in any year on record
Retractors
4/7
Proportion of countries that have decreased their ODA commitments since 2023 highs
Total DAC ODA 2025
−23.1%
Largest annual contraction on record; back to 2015 levels
Humanitarian ODA 2025
−35.8%
Second consecutive fall after five years of growth

Sources: OECD DAC Statistics. Final 2024 data released December 2025; preliminary 2025 data released April 2026. Country-level 2025 values for France, Japan, Sweden, Netherlands are author estimates.

fig = go.Figure()

for name, d in donors.items():
    # Solid markers for confirmed, open for preliminary (2024-25)
    marker_syms = ["circle" if y < 2024 else "circle-open" for y in years]
    fig.add_trace(go.Scatter(
        x=years, y=d["oda"],
        mode="lines+markers",
        name=name,
        line=dict(color=d["color"], width=2.5),
        marker=dict(color=d["color"], size=6, symbol=marker_syms, line=dict(width=2, color=d["color"])),

Billionaires, Foundations, and the Giving Pledge

Tracking the growth of private philanthropy, 2010–2025

Data Story  ·  Giving Pledge · Candid · UBS/Forbes  ·  2010–2025

In 2010, Bill Gates, Melinda French Gates, and Warren Buffett launched the Giving Pledge, asking the world’s wealthiest people to commit at least half their fortunes to charity. Fifteen years on, the number of billionaires has nearly tripled and the number of private foundations has more than doubled — but the Giving Pledge itself has lost momentum, with new sign-ups slowing to a trickle even as the fortunes it was designed to redirect keep growing.

1 — The Giving Pledge: Cumulative Signatories

The pledge grew quickly in its first decade, then stalled. Just 4 new signatories joined in 2024 — a record low — before a modest rebound to 14 in 2025. Today the pledge counts 256 signatories from roughly 30 countries, of whom about 110 are US billionaires — roughly 12% of America's billionaire population.

Cumulative signatories to the Giving Pledge by year. Compiled from the Giving Pledge public signatory list, the Institute for Policy Studies report "The Giving Pledge at 15" (2025), and contemporaneous press coverage. Figures for individual years are approximate, interpolated between known totals.

2 — Number of US Private Foundations

While headline philanthropy debates focus on a handful of billionaire names, the private foundation sector has quietly more than doubled in size since 2010 — from roughly 70,000 to over 150,000 registered foundations, most of them small family foundations rather than the mega-funds that dominate the news.

Number of private foundations registered in the United States. 2011 and 2015 figures from the Foundation Center; 2023–2025 figures from Candid. Intermediate years are estimated via smoothed interpolation.

3 — Number of Billionaires Worldwide

The billionaire population has grown in step with — and arguably faster than — both the foundation sector and the Giving Pledge. Global billionaire wealth has surged 81% since 2020 alone, reaching an estimated $18.3 trillion in 2025.

Number of individuals with net worth over $1 billion, worldwide. Source: Forbes World's Billionaires List (annual), with 2025 figure corroborated by UBS Billionaire Ambitions Report. Counts vary slightly by methodology and snapshot date.

4 — Relative Growth, Indexed to 2010

Putting all three series on the same scale (2010 = 100) shows the real story: the Giving Pledge kept pace with billionaire growth for its first decade, but new sign-ups have nearly dried up since 2023 even as billionaire wealth and the foundation sector continue to expand.

All three series indexed to 100 at 2010. This normalization shows relative growth rates rather than absolute scale — the underlying populations differ by several orders of magnitude (hundreds of signatories vs. thousands of billionaires vs. hundreds of thousands of foundations).
